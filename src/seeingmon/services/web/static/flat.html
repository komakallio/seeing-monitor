Take a flat

  1. Cover the front of the guide scope with an even light: a light panel, or a white cloth in daylight.
  2. Press Take flat, and keep the light steady.
  3. Wait until the page shows the new flat.
  4. Look at it. Press Use this flat, or Discard. Then remove the light and press Resume.
